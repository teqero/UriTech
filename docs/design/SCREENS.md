# UriGo - Design Reference

Design source: Playground da Config 2026 (Figma export)

## App Identity
- **Name:** UriGo
- **Tagline:** A sua cidade na palma da mão
- **Market:** Angola (Luanda)
- **Currency:** Kwanza (Kz)

## Screen Flow (15 pages)

### Onboarding (pages 1-4)
1. Splash - UriGo logo
2. Táxi Rápido e Seguro em Angola
3. Tudo Entregue em Minutos
4. Pague com Segurança (Multicaixa, Pagasam, Unitel Money, BAI Direto, PayPal)

### Authentication (page 5)
- Phone login (+244)
- OTP verification (6 digits)
- Account creation (name, email, province, referral code)

### Main App (pages 6-15)
- **Home:** Services grid (Taxi, Envio, Lojas, Serviços, Médico, Beleza, Imóveis, Carros, Pet Care, Partilha, Mais)
- **Taxi:** Fixed price / Bid / Rent modes, vehicle selection (Moto, Standard, Premium, Tuk-tuk)
- **Payment:** UriPay Wallet, Angola payment methods
- **Stores:** Supermarket, Pharmacy, Flowers, Water delivery
- **Intercity:** Luanda → Benguela, vehicle classes
- **Pool:** UriGo Pool ride sharing
- **Schedule:** Calendar-based trip scheduling
- **Package delivery:** Size and transport selection
- **Genie:** Personal shopping assistant
- **Ride sharing:** Find/offer rides
- **Video consultation:** Medical, legal, nutrition
- **On-demand services:** DJ, Security, Cleaning, etc.
- **Medical:** Emergency 113, doctors, ambulance
- **Beauty:** Hair, makeup, pet care
- **Marketplace:** Real estate, cars, items
